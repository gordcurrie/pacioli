Notes on CSV import
  • Account numbers in the CSV must match the account number field on your accounts.
  • Security names are matched against the name field in your securities list. Add missing securities before importing.
  • Fees, GST, withholding tax, and zero-amount distributions are skipped automatically.
  • Fund Return of Capital rows are skipped — enter ROC adjustments manually from your T3 slips.
  • Exchanges, TFSA/RRSP contributions, and unrecognized row types are flagged for manual review.
  • All imported transactions are assumed CAD. Non-CAD securities will need FX rate correction.
